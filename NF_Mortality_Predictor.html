Clinical Research Disclosure

This predictive tool is a Machine Learning (ML) research implementation developed as part of a provincial study on Necrotizing Fasciitis mortality outcomes.

Investigational Status: This application and the underlying XGBoost model are intended for research and educational purposes only. It has not been approved by Health Canada or the FDA for diagnostic or treatment use.

Clinical Judgment: Predictions should NOT be used to direct individual patient care, surgical timing, or resource prioritization. Outcomes are based on provincial registry retrospective data and represent statistical probabilities for a specific cohort.

Data Privacy: No patient-identifiable data is transmitted. All calculations occur locally on this device.
